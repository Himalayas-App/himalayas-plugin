Use the `apply-to-jobs` skill (step 5) to help the user prepare for an interview.

Job: $ARGUMENTS

1. Read the job and the user's profile, and list the questions this interviewer is most likely to ask, with what a strong answer covers.
2. Give the link to practise on Himalayas (`get_interview_practice_link`). Free accounts include one text mock interview; spoken mock interviews with an AI hiring manager are Plus (30 minutes a month).
3. If the user has practised before, summarise `get_interview_results`: the two or three things to work on.
4. Offer to run a practice round in this conversation: ask one question at a time and give feedback on each answer.
5. If the job is in the tracker, offer to move it to interviewing.
